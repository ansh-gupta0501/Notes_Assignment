# Amazon Timestream

- It is a database that is fully managed, fast , scalable and serverless for time series data.
- **Time series data?** It is data that is evolving over time. For example, in the graph , on the vertical axis we have a number, and on the horizontal axis we have the year that is evolving from an older data to newer data.(say 1996 to 2014). And so because the data is evolving over time, so it is a time series datasets.
- There is automatic scaling up and down based on capacity and the need of computes.
- You can store and analyze trillions of events per day in time series form.
- It's about a thousand times faster and 1/10th the cost of relational databases.
- If you want to analyze the time series data in real time, you could have time series analytics function to find your pattern in your database.
